\section{The masked degree test}

\begin{definition}[Masked degree test]\label{con:avss-mask}
Let $\tau,t$ and $f\in\F[X,Y]$ with $\deg_Xf,\deg_Yf<2^\tau$ be as in \cref{def:avss}. The
dealer samples $e$ uniformly from $V_t$ and commits to $\hat e$ with salted Merkle leaves: the
leaf of $x$ hashes $x$, $\hat e(x)$ and an independent uniform salt. The verifier sends
$\theta\in\F$. Put $h:=f+\theta e$ and $u_h:=u_f+\theta u_e$. The two parties run
$\Pi_{w^X,t}$ (\cref{def:protocol}) with $k=2$, $m=2\tau$, $D=t$ and $u_0:=u_h$, the verifier
reading $u_h(x)$ by opening $u_f(x)$ and $u_e(x)$; the $s$
repetitions use as query points $\xi_0$ the $s$ query positions at which the commitment already opens $\hat f$, in order, so that $s$ equals the number of queries of the commitment and each $\xi_0$ is uniform on $L_0$.
\end{definition}
\uses{\cref{def:avss,def:protocol}}\usedby{\cref{lem:avss-bad,lem:avss-dist,prop:avss-sound,lem:avss-secret}}

\begin{remark}[The threshold is not enforced]\label{rem:avss-threshold}
Let $2^\tau\ge t+2$ and let $f\in\F[X,Y]$ satisfy $\deg_Xf,\deg_Yf<2^\tau$ and
$\deg_Xf(X,0)>t$; for instance $f=X^{t+1}$ if $t+1\le2^\tau-1$. PolyFRIM's binding certifies
only $\deg_Xf,\deg_Yf<2^\tau$ {[PolyFRIM, Def.~2.3, App.~B, App.~D]}, and the dealer's messages
and proofs are those of an honest dealer for this $f$, so every check of FRISS
{[PolyFRIM, Alg.~2--4]} passes. The Rec\_strong value of party $i$ is $\sigma_i:=f(\omega_i,0)$, at
distinct points $\omega_i\in\F$ {[PolyFRIM, Alg.~3--4]}. For a set $A$ of $t+1$ parties let
$P_A\in\F[X]$ be the interpolant of $\{(\omega_i,\sigma_i):\ i\in A\}$, of degree at most $t$, and put
$\sigma:=f(z,0)$. Then one of two cases occurs.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P_A\ne P_B$ for some two sets $A,B$ of $t+1$ parties. Then $P_A-P_B$ is a nonzero
polynomial of degree at most $t$, so $P_A(z)\ne P_B(z)$ for every $z$ outside a set of at most
$t$ elements: the parties may output different secrets.
\item $P_A=P$ for every $A$. Then $P$ agrees with $f(\cdot,0)$ on every $\omega_i$, while
$P\ne f(\cdot,0)$ because $\deg f(\cdot,0)>t\ge\deg P$; hence $f(\cdot,0)-P$ is a nonzero
polynomial of degree at most $2^\tau-1$, and $P(z)\ne\sigma$ for every $z$ outside its set of at
most $2^\tau-1$ roots: every party outputs a secret other than $s$.
\end{enumerate}
The same argument applies with $X$ and $Y$ exchanged, to the shares $\hat f_i=f(\omega_i,Y)$
interpolated from $t+1$ valid Echo values {[PolyFRIM, Alg.~3, step 16]}.
\end{remark}

This does not contradict {[PolyFRIM, Thm.~4.1]}: Definition~2.4 there constrains the
reconstructed value only when the dealer is honest (Correctness), and its Agreement property
requires that all honest parties terminate, not that they output the same value. The proof of Correctness in {[PolyFRIM, App.~E]} goes further and states, without assuming an honest
dealer, that the honest parties reconstruct the same secret. \cref{rem:avss-threshold} shows
that this stronger property, that a malicious dealer be bound to a single secret, does not hold
without a degree test.

\begin{lemma}[One bad challenge]\label{lem:avss-bad}
Let $f\in\F[X,Y]$, $e\in\F[X,Y]$ with $\deg_Xe,\deg_Ye<2^\tau$, and $\deg_Xf>t$. Then $\deg_X(f+\theta e)\le t$ for at most one
$\theta\in\F$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}\begin{enumerate}
\item Assume $\deg_X(f+\theta e)\le t$ and $\deg_X(f+\theta'e)\le t$ for some
$\theta\ne\theta'$.
\item Then $(\theta-\theta')e=(f+\theta e)-(f+\theta'e)$ has $\deg_X\le t$, and
$\theta-\theta'\ne0$, so $\deg_Xe\le t$.
\item Hence $\deg_Xf=\deg_X\big((f+\theta e)-\theta e\big)\le t$, contradicting the hypothesis.
\end{enumerate}\end{proof}
